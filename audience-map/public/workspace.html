Upload sales

CSV only, up to 5 MiB / 10000 rows. No names, emails or individual profiles are accepted.

Columns: sale_id, sold_on, municipality_code, tickets, revenue. Dates: YYYY-MM-DD. Currency: BRL with decimal point. Blank origins are allowed but remain unresolved. Corrections to the same event/edition/sale ID append a version; omitted rows are retained.

Actual sales and editions

Compare uploaded editions and the recorded origins of buyers. Missing uploads do not mean zero sales.

Saved scenarios and reports

Create a scenario with “Save current scenario” on the map. Captures preserve the complete filtered scope, source IDs and versions; viewport and zoom are not saved as population filters.

Opportunity hypotheses

Choose a saved scenario and the event selected in Sales. Rank locations by their census cohort and the presence of uploaded tickets. This is exploratory prioritization, not a prediction of demand.
